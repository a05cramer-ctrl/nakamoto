02 · held by no one

A Bitcoin address
with no private key.

The reserve address comes from NEAR Chain Signatures. Its key is split into shares across NEAR's validator network. They sign together when the contract says so, and the full key is never put together anywhere. Not by the dev, not by a server, not by anyone.

DEMONSTRATION · real secp256k1 math in your browser · not the reserve address
bitcoin address press generate private key —
  1. 8 nodes each roll a secret share. It never leaves the node.
  2. Each node publishes only its public share, a point on the curve.
  3. Your browser adds the 8 public points and turns the sum into a real bc1 address.
  4. The private key for that address would be the sum of all 8 secrets. No one ever holds all 8, so the key exists nowhere.
